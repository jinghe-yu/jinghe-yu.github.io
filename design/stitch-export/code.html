EmoMirror: Negotiable Affective Mirroring for Students with Elevated Alexithymia

Authors: Jinghe Yu*, Dong She*, Xinyao Li, Fanbo Meng, Yang Gao, Zhanpeng Jin

Individuals with elevated alexithymia experience fundamental hurdles in identifying and articulating their affective states. EmoMirror introduces a negotiable affective mirroring interactive system designed for college students, enabling voluntary, non-coercive visual feedback that fosters cognitive reflection and emotional literacy without imposing rigid diagnostic labels.

CHI 2026 Posters Published Co-First Author

EmoType-Zero: Adaptive Fonts to Render the Speech Emotion

Authors: Dong She*, Jinghe Yu*, Nuo Chen, Yuting Bai, Yang Gao, Zhanpeng Jin

Speech carries critical prosodic and emotional nuance that traditional static typography fails to reflect in written communication. EmoType-Zero synthesizes dynamic, adaptive fonts directly from acoustic speech inputs, modulating typography attributes in real-time according to vocal emotional valence and arousal.

Touch Through Sound: Ring-Mounted Auditory Pseudo-Haptics for Material Perception

Authors: Zhengte Cai, Chiyue Wang, Yumiao Wang, Jinghe Yu, Chen Li, Zhanpeng Jin, Yang Gao

Explores ring-mounted auditory pseudo-haptics to render continuous tactile material perceptions without requiring bulky mechanical actuators.
